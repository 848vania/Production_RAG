# Remote Work Policy
**Nexora Technologies, Inc.**
**Version 2.1 | Effective Date: March 1, 2026**

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements at Nexora Technologies, Inc. ("Nexora" or "the Company"). It applies to all full-time and part-time employees who work outside a Nexora office location on a regular or occasional basis.

Remote work is a privilege, not an entitlement. Eligibility depends on role requirements, performance standing, and business needs. The Company reserves the right to modify or revoke remote work arrangements at any time.

---

## 2. Work Models

Nexora operates under three defined work models:

### 2.1 Hybrid (Default)
Most employees follow the hybrid model: a minimum of 2 days per week in the nearest Nexora office, with the remaining days worked remotely. In-office days are coordinated at the team level and must be agreed upon with the direct manager. Mondays and Wednesdays are designated anchor days for most teams.

### 2.2 Fully Remote
A small number of roles have been designated as fully remote. These roles are identified in the job description at time of hire. Fully remote employees are required to travel to a Nexora office for quarterly team on-sites (travel expenses are reimbursed per the Expense Policy).

### 2.3 Fully On-Site
Certain roles—including facilities, lab operations, and select customer support functions—require full-time on-site presence. Employees in these roles are not eligible for remote work arrangements unless granted a temporary exception.

---

## 3. Eligibility

To be eligible for any remote work arrangement, an employee must:

- Have completed at least 90 days of employment
- Be in good standing (no active Performance Improvement Plan)
- Have a role that does not require physical presence to perform essential functions
- Have appropriate home workspace conditions (see Section 5)
- Have manager and People Operations approval

New hires are expected to work on-site full-time for the first 90 days unless the role was explicitly designated as remote at the time of offer.

---

## 4. Approved Work Locations

### 4.1 Primary Remote Location
Employees must designate a primary remote work location (city and state/province) and notify People Operations of any change. Tax withholding and compliance obligations are determined by work location, and unauthorized changes can create legal and tax complications for both the employee and the Company.

### 4.2 Temporary Location Changes
Employees may work from a location other than their primary remote location for up to 20 business days per calendar year without prior approval, provided the location is within the United States or Canada. Extended stays or international locations require written approval from the employee's manager and the Legal team at least 30 days in advance.

### 4.3 International Remote Work
Nexora does not generally authorize employees to work from countries outside the US and Canada. Exceptions may be considered for up to 10 consecutive business days for personal reasons (e.g., visiting family) and require Legal and People Operations approval. Unauthorized international remote work may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 5. Home Workspace Requirements

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a workspace that supports productive, secure, and professional work. The workspace must:

- Have a reliable, private internet connection with minimum speeds of 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload
- Be free from excessive noise and interruptions during meetings and calls
- Allow the employee to maintain data confidentiality (e.g., no family members or guests visible during video calls involving confidential matters)
- Be ergonomically set up to minimize injury risk

Nexora provides a one-time home office stipend of $800 for employees approved for hybrid or fully remote work to purchase equipment such as a monitor, desk chair, or keyboard. This stipend must be approved in advance, spent within 90 days of approval, and submitted through Workday. Equipment purchased with this stipend is company property.

---

## 6. Availability and Communication

### 6.1 Core Hours
Remote employees must be available and responsive during core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone, Monday through Friday.

### 6.2 Response Time Expectations
- Slack messages: respond within 2 hours during core hours
- Email: respond within 4 hours during core hours or same business day
- Meeting invitations: respond (accept/decline) within 24 hours of receipt

### 6.3 Status and Visibility
Employees must keep their Slack status current (available, in a meeting, away, etc.) and update their calendar to reflect their schedule accurately. Unplanned absences or extended unavailability during core hours must be communicated to the manager as soon as possible.

### 6.4 Video Presence
Video cameras are expected to be on during team meetings, 1:1s with managers, and any meeting with fewer than 10 participants, unless the employee has a technical issue or prior arrangement with the meeting organizer.

---

## 7. Security Requirements

Remote employees must follow all requirements in the Nexora Security Policy and IT Onboarding Guide. Key obligations include:

- Use only company-issued or approved devices for work
- Connect to the Nexora VPN when accessing internal systems
- Use a password-protected Wi-Fi network; do not use public or unencrypted Wi-Fi without VPN
- Lock device screen immediately when stepping away from the workspace
- Do not allow household members or guests to use company devices
- Report lost or stolen devices to IT Security immediately at security@nexora-internal.com or 1-866-NEX-SECU

---

## 8. Expense Reimbursement

### 8.1 Internet
Nexora reimburses up to $60 per month for home internet service for employees in hybrid or fully remote roles. Submit receipts monthly through Workday with the "Internet — Remote Work" category.

### 8.2 Phone
Employees who use their personal phone for work may claim up to $30 per month for business-related mobile use. A business-use justification is required.

### 8.3 Co-Working Spaces
Employees who prefer to work from a co-working space rather than home may expense up to $250 per month for a co-working membership, subject to manager approval. Drop-in day passes may be expensed for up to $30 per day, up to 5 days per month.

### 8.4 On-Site Travel for Remote Employees
Fully remote employees required to travel to a Nexora office for on-sites or business purposes are reimbursed for reasonable travel expenses in accordance with the Expense Policy.

---

## 9. Performance Accountability

Remote work does not change performance expectations. Employees are evaluated on output and results, not hours logged. Managers are responsible for setting clear goals and check-in cadences for remote team members. A pattern of missed deliverables, unresponsiveness, or failure to attend required meetings may result in revocation of remote work privileges.

---

## 10. Equipment and Technical Support

Remote employees have the same access to IT support as on-site employees. Technical issues should be submitted via the IT Help Desk at helpdesk.nexora-internal.com or by emailing ithelp@nexora-internal.com. For urgent issues affecting the ability to work, call the IT Hotline at 1-866-NEX-TECH (available business hours, Monday–Friday).

---

## 11. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy—including unauthorized work locations, failure to maintain availability, or security non-compliance—may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of remote work privileges or employment.

---

## 12. Amendments

This policy is reviewed annually by People Operations and Legal. The most current version is available at wiki.nexora-internal.com/hr/remote-work-policy.
