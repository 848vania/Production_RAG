# Data Privacy Policy
**Nexora Technologies, Inc.**
**Version 2.3 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

Nexora Technologies, Inc. ("Nexora," "we," "our," or "the Company") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal data. This Data Privacy Policy describes how Nexora collects, uses, stores, discloses, and disposes of personal data in the context of its internal operations and in the delivery of its services to customers.

This policy applies to:
- All Nexora employees, contractors, and temporary workers
- All personal data processed by Nexora in connection with employment and operations
- Customer data processed by Nexora on behalf of its enterprise clients

This policy is complementary to, and should be read alongside, the Information Security Policy and the Vendor Management Policy.

Questions about data privacy may be directed to the Data Privacy Officer at privacy@nexora-internal.com.

---

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Personal Data** | Any information that can identify a natural person directly or indirectly (e.g., name, email, IP address, device ID) |
| **Sensitive Personal Data** | A subset of personal data requiring heightened protection: race/ethnicity, health data, biometrics, religion, sexual orientation, financial account data, government IDs |
| **Data Subject** | The identified or identifiable natural person whose data is processed |
| **Data Controller** | The entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data |
| **Data Processor** | An entity that processes data on behalf of a controller |
| **Processing** | Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, transfer, deletion) |

For customer data, Nexora acts as a Data Processor. For employee data, Nexora acts as a Data Controller.

---

## 3. Personal Data We Collect

### 3.1 Employee Data
In connection with employment, Nexora collects:
- Identity data: full name, date of birth, national ID or SSN (where required)
- Contact data: home address, personal email, personal phone number
- Employment data: job title, department, manager, salary, benefits elections
- Performance data: reviews, disciplinary records, training completions
- Technical data: device identifiers, access logs, authentication records
- Payroll and benefits data: bank account details, tax withholding information, health plan enrollment

### 3.2 Customer Data
Nexora processes personal data on behalf of its enterprise customers as part of its platform services. The types of data processed depend on the customer's configuration and use case, but may include names, email addresses, financial transaction data, and other operational data belonging to the customer's employees or end users. Nexora processes this data only as instructed by the customer under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

### 3.3 Website and Marketing Data
Nexora collects limited personal data from visitors to its public websites, including IP addresses, browser information, and form submission data. This collection is governed by Nexora's external Privacy Notice, available at nexoratech.com/privacy.

---

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

Nexora processes personal data only when it has a lawful basis to do so. Applicable legal bases include:

- **Contract**: Processing necessary to fulfill employment obligations or customer agreements
- **Legal Obligation**: Processing required to comply with applicable law (e.g., tax reporting, employment law)
- **Legitimate Interests**: Processing for Nexora's operational needs that do not override individual rights (e.g., security monitoring, fraud prevention)
- **Consent**: Processing based on clear, informed, and voluntary consent (obtained for specific purposes such as optional wellness programs)

Sensitive personal data is only processed where a specific additional legal basis applies (e.g., explicit consent, employment law obligations).

---

## 5. Data Use and Purpose Limitation

Personal data must be used only for the purpose for which it was collected. Employees who handle personal data must:

- Use data only for the specific business task at hand
- Not repurpose or reuse data for analytics, testing, or secondary purposes without Privacy team approval
- Not combine data sets in ways that were not disclosed to data subjects without a documented legitimate basis

Use of customer personal data for training internal machine learning models is prohibited without explicit customer consent and Privacy team sign-off.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Personal data must not be retained longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Nexora's data retention schedule governs minimum and maximum retention periods for each data category. Key retention periods include:

| Data Category | Retention Period |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Employee records (active) | Duration of employment + 7 years |
| Payroll and tax records | 7 years after tax year end |
| Recruitment records (not hired) | 2 years |
| Customer data (post-contract) | As specified in DPA, default 90 days |
| Security and access logs | 1 year |
| Website analytics | 13 months |

Data that has reached its retention limit must be securely deleted in accordance with the Information Security Policy. Exceptions due to legal holds must be approved by Legal.

---

## 7. Data Subject Rights

Nexora respects the rights of data subjects to control their personal data. Depending on applicable law, individuals may have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of the personal data Nexora holds about them
- **Correction**: Request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of personal data (subject to legal and contractual obligations)
- **Restriction**: Request that processing be limited in certain circumstances
- **Portability**: Receive personal data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

Employees may submit rights requests to the People Operations team. Customers and other data subjects should submit requests to privacy@nexora-internal.com. All valid requests must be acknowledged within 5 business days and fulfilled within 30 days, extendable by an additional 60 days for complex requests.

---

## 8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 8.1 Internal Sharing
Access to personal data is restricted to employees with a legitimate business need. Data must not be shared more broadly than necessary within the Company.

### 8.2 Third-Party Processors
Nexora may share personal data with approved third-party service providers (e.g., payroll processor, benefits administrator, cloud infrastructure providers) who act as data processors. All such providers must:
- Be subject to a Data Processing Agreement meeting Nexora's standards
- Provide sufficient security guarantees
- Process data only on Nexora's documented instructions

The full list of approved sub-processors is maintained by the Privacy team and reviewed quarterly.

### 8.3 Legal Disclosures
Nexora may disclose personal data when required by law, court order, or regulatory authority. Legal team approval is required before disclosing in response to any governmental request.

### 8.4 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity subject to equivalent privacy protections.

---

## 9. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Nexora operates in the United States and Canada. Transfers of personal data originating from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland must comply with applicable transfer mechanisms (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses). Transfers to unapproved countries require Privacy team sign-off. The Privacy team maintains a register of all cross-border transfers.

---

## 10. Privacy by Design

Privacy considerations must be incorporated into all new products, features, processes, and systems from the outset—not added after the fact. Any new initiative involving personal data must complete a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) before launch. PIA templates are available at wiki.nexora-internal.com/privacy/pia. The Privacy team must review and approve PIAs for high-risk processing activities.

---

## 11. Data Breach Response

A personal data breach is any incident that results in unauthorized access to, disclosure of, loss of, or destruction of personal data. All suspected breaches must be reported immediately to the Security team and Privacy Officer. The incident response process is governed by the Incident Response Policy. For breaches involving EU/UK personal data, regulatory notification may be required within 72 hours of discovery.

---

## 12. Training and Accountability

All employees who handle personal data must complete privacy awareness training at hire and annually thereafter. Employees in roles with significant data processing responsibilities (Engineering, Product, Customer Success) receive role-specific privacy training.

The Data Privacy Officer maintains records of processing activities, manages rights requests, coordinates with regulators, and reports to the General Counsel and CEO.

---

## 13. Policy Updates

This policy is reviewed at least annually by the Privacy team and Legal. Material updates are communicated to employees via email and posted on the internal wiki at wiki.nexora-internal.com/legal/data-privacy-policy.
