# Expense Reimbursement Policy
**Nexora Technologies, Inc.**
**Version 3.0 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026**

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the reimbursement of business expenses incurred by Nexora Technologies, Inc. ("Nexora" or "the Company") employees in the course of performing their job responsibilities. It is designed to ensure that business expenses are reasonable, necessary, properly documented, and approved before reimbursement is issued.

All employees who incur business expenses on behalf of the Company are subject to this policy. Questions should be directed to Finance at expenses@nexora-internal.com.

---

## 2. General Principles

- **Spend wisely**: Employees should spend company money as they would their own—economically and without waste.
- **Necessary and appropriate**: Expenses must be directly related to a legitimate business purpose.
- **Pre-approval for large spend**: Expenses over $500 require manager pre-approval before they are incurred.
- **Timely submission**: All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed without a written exception approved by the Controller.
- **No personal benefit**: Expenses that primarily benefit the employee personally are not reimbursable.

---

## 3. Expense Submission Process

Employees submit expense reports through Nexora's expense management platform, **Expensify**, accessible at expenses.nexora-internal.com or via the Expensify mobile app.

Each expense submission must include:
1. A clear, legible receipt (photo or PDF)
2. Business purpose (who, what, why)
3. Attendees for meal and entertainment expenses
4. Manager approval (auto-routed in Expensify for amounts over $100)

Receipts are required for all individual expenses of $25 or more. For expenses under $25, a written description in lieu of a receipt is acceptable.

Approved reimbursements are processed on the 15th and last business day of each month and paid via direct deposit.

---

## 4. Travel Expenses

### 4.1 Air Travel
Employees must book flights in **economy class** for domestic travel and flights up to 6 hours in duration. Business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 6 hours with manager approval. Tickets must be purchased at least 14 days in advance when possible. Nexora's preferred booking portal is TravelNexora (travel.nexora-internal.com). Booking outside the portal requires a written explanation.

Upgrades using personal frequent flyer miles or credit card points are permitted and do not require reimbursement. Upgrades purchased with company funds require VP-level approval.

### 4.2 Hotels
Nightly hotel rates are subject to the following per-diem limits (excluding taxes and fees):

| City Tier | Per-Night Limit |
|-----------|----------------|
| Tier 1 (New York, San Francisco, Boston, Washington D.C.) | $275 |
| Tier 2 (Chicago, Los Angeles, Seattle, Austin, Miami) | $225 |
| Tier 3 (All other domestic cities) | $175 |
| International | $300 |

Employees should book through TravelNexora to access Nexora's negotiated rates. If a suitable hotel within the per-diem limit is unavailable (e.g., during a major conference), the employee must document the search and seek manager approval before booking.

Nexora does not reimburse resort fees, minibar charges, in-room movies, or spa services.

### 4.3 Ground Transportation
- **Rental cars**: Economy or compact class only, unless 3+ employees are traveling together. Employees must decline the rental agency's collision damage waiver (CDW)—Nexora's corporate insurance provides coverage for business-use rentals.
- **Rideshare and taxis**: Reimbursable for business travel. A business justification is required if the total trip exceeds $75.
- **Personal vehicle**: Reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate for business use. Mileage must be logged in Expensify, including origin, destination, and business purpose.
- **Public transit**: Fully reimbursable with receipt or fare documentation.
- **Parking**: Reimbursable for business-related parking. Long-term airport parking should be economy/off-site where available.

### 4.4 Per Diem for Meals During Travel
When employees are traveling overnight on company business, a per-diem meal allowance applies:

| Meal | Domestic | International |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Breakfast | $20 | $25 |
| Lunch | $25 | $30 |
| Dinner | $55 | $70 |
| **Daily Total** | **$100** | **$125** |

Per diems are reduced proportionally for partial travel days. Receipts are not required for meals within the per-diem limit. Expenses exceeding the per-diem require itemized receipts and manager approval.

## 5. Meals and Entertainment

### 5.1 Business Meals
Meals with clients, prospects, or business partners are reimbursable when there is a clear business purpose. The business purpose and names of all attendees must be recorded. Nexora does not reimburse alcohol separately; one alcoholic drink per person is included in the meal limit.

Per-person meal limits (excluding tax and tip):
- Breakfast or lunch with external guests: $50/person
- Dinner with external guests: $100/person
- Internal team meals (manager-approved): $40/person

Tip of up to 20% is reimbursable and does not count toward the per-person limit.

### 5.2 Client Entertainment
Client entertainment (sporting events, concerts, golf, etc.) must be pre-approved by the VP of Sales or VP of Customer Success before the expense is incurred. Tickets or fees may not exceed $300 per person per event. The business purpose, client name, and account name must be documented.

### 5.3 Team Events
Department or team celebration events (e.g., quarterly dinners, offsites) require VP-level pre-approval. Budget guidance is provided by Finance on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 6. Software and Subscriptions

Employees may not subscribe to or renew software tools, SaaS products, or online services using company funds without prior approval from their manager and the IT department. All software purchases are subject to IT security review. Unapproved software purchases will not be reimbursed.

For approved subscriptions, employees should use the company purchasing card where possible rather than personal cards.

---

## 7. Office Supplies and Equipment

- Office supplies under $50: Reimbursable with receipt and manager approval.
- Equipment or supplies over $50: Require manager pre-approval and must be ordered through Nexora's preferred supplier (OfficeSource Pro) where available.
- Equipment over $500: Require VP approval and are capitalized as company assets.

---

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:

- Personal entertainment, hobbies, or recreational activities
- Traffic and parking fines
- Lost baggage or personal item loss (employees may file insurance claims directly)
- Personal travel extensions to business trips
- Dry cleaning or personal grooming (except for trips exceeding 5 consecutive days)
- Gifts to employees (use the Gift Card program through People Ops)
- Political donations or contributions
- Alcohol beyond the per-person meal allowance
- First-class airfare (except with C-level approval for medical necessity)
- Personal use of rental vehicles

---

## 9. Corporate Credit Cards

Senior employees at the Manager level and above may be issued a Nexora corporate credit card (administered through FirstEdge Bank). Corporate card usage must comply with this policy. Cardholders must reconcile card transactions in Expensify within 15 days of each statement close. Late reconciliation may result in card suspension.

Misuse of the corporate card—including personal charges, unauthorized purchases, or failure to reconcile—may result in disciplinary action and personal liability for unauthorized charges.

---

## 10. Policy Violations

Submitting false expense reports or misrepresenting business purposes is grounds for immediate termination and may result in legal action. Finance reviews expense reports for compliance and may flag anomalies for management review.
