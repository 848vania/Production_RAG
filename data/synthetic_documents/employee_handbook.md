# Employee Handbook
**Nexora Technologies, Inc.**
**Version 4.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026**

---

## 1. Welcome to Nexora

Welcome to Nexora Technologies, Inc. ("Nexora" or "the Company"). We are a B2B software company headquartered in Austin, Texas, with offices in Chicago, New York, and Toronto. Our platform helps mid-market and enterprise clients automate financial operations workflows.

This handbook outlines our policies, expectations, and the resources available to you as a Nexora employee. All employees are expected to read this handbook in full during their first week of employment and to comply with its contents throughout their tenure.

Questions about this handbook should be directed to the People Operations team at people@nexora-internal.com.

---

## 2. Employment Classifications

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees
Employees who work a minimum of 40 hours per week on a regular schedule. Full-time employees are eligible for the full benefits package described in Section 7.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees
Employees who work fewer than 30 hours per week. Part-time employees are eligible for prorated vacation accrual and may qualify for limited health benefits depending on hours worked.

### 2.3 Contractors and Consultants
Individuals engaged through a services agreement who are not employees of Nexora. Contractors are not eligible for employee benefits and are subject to their individual contracts rather than this handbook.

### 2.4 Temporary Employees
Employees hired for a defined period (not to exceed 12 months) through a staffing agency or direct engagement. Temporary employees are not eligible for standard benefits.

## 3. Code of Conduct

### 3.1 Professional Standards
All employees are expected to conduct themselves with honesty, integrity, and professionalism at all times—whether in the office, working remotely, traveling on company business, or representing Nexora at external events.

### 3.2 Respectful Workplace
Nexora is committed to a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, and retaliation. This commitment applies to all employees regardless of race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

Harassment includes, but is not limited to:
- Unwelcome sexual advances or comments
- Repeated requests for personal relationships after a clear refusal
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or derogatory language
- Intimidation or bullying
- Deliberate exclusion from meetings or communications

Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it immediately to their manager, the People Operations team, or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1-888-395-7742.

### 3.3 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any potential conflict of interest to their manager and the Legal team before engaging in outside business activities, serving on boards, or entering into transactions with entities that do business with Nexora. Annual conflict-of-interest disclosure forms are distributed each December and must be returned by January 15.

### 3.4 Confidentiality
Employees have access to confidential business information including trade secrets, customer data, financial projections, and product roadmaps. This information must not be shared with unauthorized parties inside or outside the Company, and this obligation continues after employment ends. Refer to the Data Privacy Policy for additional requirements.

## 4. Work Hours and Scheduling

### 4.1 Standard Hours
The standard workweek at Nexora is Monday through Friday, with core hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in each employee's local time zone. Employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration during core hours. Scheduling outside these hours requires manager approval.

### 4.2 Flexible Scheduling
Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their schedules to accommodate personal needs, provided that work is completed on time and team commitments are met. Flexible scheduling arrangements should be discussed with and approved by the direct manager.

### 4.3 Overtime
Non-exempt employees who work more than 40 hours in a workweek are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5× their regular rate. Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by a manager. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay.

## 5. Time Off Policies

### 5.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
Nexora uses a unified PTO bank that covers vacation, personal days, and non-medical appointments. PTO accruals are as follows:

| Years of Service | Annual Accrual | Maximum Carryover |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 0–2 years       | 15 days        | 10 days           |
| 3–5 years       | 20 days        | 15 days           |
| 6+ years        | 25 days        | 20 days           |

PTO requests should be submitted in Workday at least 5 business days in advance for requests of 1–3 days, and at least 15 business days in advance for requests of 4 or more consecutive days. Approval is at the manager's discretion based on business needs.

### 5.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not roll over. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required. Sick leave may also be used to care for an ill immediate family member.

### 5.3 Company Holidays
Nexora observes the following paid holidays each year:

- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth National Independence Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve (half day)
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve (half day)

When a holiday falls on a weekend, the preceding Friday or following Monday is observed.

### 5.4 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or placement of a child. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave must commence within 12 months of the qualifying event and must be coordinated with the People Operations team at least 60 days in advance when possible.

### 5.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid bereavement days upon the death of an immediate family member (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, or grandparent) and up to 3 days for extended family members (aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, in-law).

## 6. Performance and Development

### 6.1 Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year—in June and December. Reviews are managed in Lattice. Employees receive written feedback from their direct manager and, where applicable, peer feedback from colleagues selected jointly by the employee and manager.

### 6.2 Performance Improvement Plans
If an employee's performance does not meet expectations, the manager and People Operations may place the employee on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP defines specific, measurable objectives and a timeline (typically 30 to 90 days) for achieving them. Failure to meet PIP requirements may result in termination.

### 6.3 Learning and Development
Nexora provides each full-time employee a $1,500 annual learning stipend for external courses, certifications, conferences, or books relevant to their role. Requests must be approved by the manager and submitted through the L&D portal in Workday before purchase. Unused stipend does not carry over to the next calendar year.

### 6.4 Internal Mobility
After 12 months in a role, employees may apply for internal job openings. Employees must notify their current manager of their application within 5 business days of applying. The hiring team will coordinate with People Operations to manage any transition.

## 7. Benefits

### 7.1 Health Insurance
Nexora offers medical, dental, and vision coverage through BluePeak Health Group. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the employee's start date. The Company covers 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage and 70% for dependent coverage. Plan details and enrollment are managed through the Benefitsync portal.

### 7.2 401(k) Retirement Plan
Employees are eligible to participate in Nexora's 401(k) plan beginning on their first day of employment. Nexora matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary. The match vests on a 3-year graded schedule (33% at year 1, 66% at year 2, 100% at year 3).

### 7.3 Equity
Eligible employees receive Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) as part of their total compensation. RSUs vest over a 4-year schedule with a 1-year cliff. Equity details are outlined in each employee's offer letter and the Equity Plan agreement.

### 7.4 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees and their household members have access to confidential counseling, financial guidance, and legal consultation through the Nexora EAP, administered by ClearMind Wellness. Up to 8 free sessions per issue per year are available. Contact: 1-800-482-9100 or eap.nexora.clearmind.com.

### 7.5 Wellness Reimbursement
Employees receive up to $600 per year for eligible wellness expenses, including gym memberships, fitness equipment, meditation apps, or mental health apps not covered by the EAP. Submit receipts through Workday with the "Wellness Reimbursement" category.

## 8. Technology and Equipment

### 8.1 Company-Issued Equipment
All full-time employees receive a company-issued laptop, headset, and peripherals. Equipment must be returned upon separation from the Company in good working condition, excluding reasonable wear and tear.

### 8.2 Acceptable Use
Company equipment and networks are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted, but employees should have no expectation of privacy on company systems. The IT department may monitor activity on company networks and devices in accordance with applicable law.

### 8.3 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
Personal devices used to access Nexora systems must have the Nexora MDM (Mobile Device Management) profile installed and comply with all requirements in the IT Onboarding Guide and Security Policy.

---

## 9. Separation from Employment

### 9.1 Voluntary Resignation
Employees wishing to resign are asked to provide a minimum of 2 weeks' written notice. Senior leadership and individual contributors in key technical roles are encouraged to provide 4 weeks when possible. Final pay will be issued on the next regular payday following separation.

### 9.2 Involuntary Termination
The Company may terminate employment at any time, with or without cause, in accordance with applicable law. Employees terminated without cause will receive severance as outlined in their employment agreement.

### 9.3 Return of Property
Upon separation, all company property—including equipment, access badges, and physical documents—must be returned on or before the last day of employment. Failure to return property may result in the fair market value being deducted from the final paycheck to the extent permitted by law.

### 9.4 Exit Interviews
The People Operations team may conduct an exit interview to gather feedback. Participation is voluntary.
